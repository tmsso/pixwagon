Annotation 06

Thumb reach is a layout rule, not a nicety

Every control here is at least 2.75rem tall, and each screen's primary action sits in the lower half — the top half carries state, the bottom half carries actions.

Consequence for Home: the name field can sit high because it is optional on return; the three mode buttons cannot.

Annotation 01

BoardCell carries six states, and one of them is a rollback

blank · fillable · candidate · filled · locked · invalid. invalid is the visible undo when the referee rejects an optimistic fill — it must read as "that was taken back", never as a mistake the player made.

Hence the wording in the third frame: it names who got there first instead of scolding.

Annotation 03

The board scales in whole pixels

It renders on Canvas with integer scaling and no smoothing, so cell size steps rather than glides between phone and desktop. No soft shadows on cells, no fractional sizes, no per-cell rounding beyond 0.

Annotation 07 · revision

DiceFace combos stack onto two lines

The combination faces (1+2 · 2+2 · 1+3) previously ran as one inline row — blob, +, blob — which crowded the 48px face at md and clipped at sm.

Each blob now takes its own line, separated by a hairline rule in --border. Two blobs still read as two, the + glyph is no longer needed, and the face is comfortable at all three sizes.

Annotation 04

Disabled beats hidden

Between submitting a fill and the referee's answer, the control stays in place at the same height and dims. Hiding it would collapse the footer and shift the board at the exact moment the player is watching it.

Same reason the no legal move state keeps both offers on screen and adds a pass action instead of emptying the footer.

Annotation 02

Colour is never the only signal

The six seat colours are Okabe–Ito derived and each carries a hatch: solid, diagonal, dots, horizontal, cross, vertical. Every swatch on every surface — chips, ranks, filled cells — keeps the hatch.

The same rule covers state: an absent player is dimmed and labelled "away"; a selected pack is ticked and outlined.

Annotation 08

Offline is a first-class state, not an error

Solo and daily run with no network at all, so no flow in them may depend on a green connection indicator. The connection pill exists only inside a room, and reads Connecting… in warning tone rather than red.

Equally: nothing on any surface implies a profile, a login, or a saved history beyond "the daily you already did today".
